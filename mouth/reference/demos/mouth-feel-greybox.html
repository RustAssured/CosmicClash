MOUTH

FEEL PROTOTYPE · 60 FPS · GREYBOX

Same creature, drawn plainly so it runs smoothly — this is for judging how it feels, not how it looks. You only slide the body along the edge; the head never stops watching its prey. Choose high, straight or low, then strike. The neck's shape is authored for every beat — coiled in the wind-up, an arc in the strike — and the rope only adds weight and lag on top.

Keyboard (click the canvas): A/D slide · W/S high / low (release for straight) · J bite · K sweep · L coil & crush. A gamepad works too: left stick slides, up/down chooses height, A / X / B strike. Drag the opponent to place it.
