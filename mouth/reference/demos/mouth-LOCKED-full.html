MOUTH

THE FEEL, IN FULL · YOUR TUNING

The greybox's motion — your tuning — now driving the full look: the coils sliding along the edge, the braided neck folding in the wind-up and arcing in the strike, the jaw-rings spinning up and clamping. High, straight and low are now a real choice: high arcs wide over the top and reaches furthest but is slowest; low comes in under, quicker but shorter; and in the wind-up both of you can see the path it will take. The simulation runs at a steady 60 per second; the drawing is the CPU look-development renderer, so it shows fewer frames than the game will.

Keyboard (click the canvas): A/D slide · W/S high / low (release for straight) · J bite · K sweep · L coil & crush. A gamepad works too: left stick slides, up/down chooses height, A / X / B strike. Drag the opponent to place it; click anywhere on the Mouth to hit it there — the stretched neck after a strike takes extra damage.
