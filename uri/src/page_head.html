Moves are no longer poses but intentions: a target, an arm, a commitment. Force enters at the centre of mass and travels outward — the root shifts, the pylons counter-thrust, the torso turns, the shoulder ark rolls, the arm unfolds, the fist arrives last. Every link has mass, stiffness, damping and limits; the arm's momentum pushes the rest of the body the other way, the pylons fire to solve it, the loop stretches and shears under the load, the command moon holds its own position. The fists draw their pieces together as they commit. Damage changes how it settles; break a connection and what hangs from it drifts away.
