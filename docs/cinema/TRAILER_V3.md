# ADEUK: TITANS: "우리" launch trailer (v3): master build prompt

Source: WJ, 2026-10-10. This is the brief for cinema mode. Progress is logged in `docs/cinema/PROGRESS.md`.

Build the launch trailer **inside the game itself**. Do not edit it in a video editor.

The story trailer (v2) proved the story. The gameplay montage proved the look. v3 fuses the two: every frame comes from the real engine, with the real titans and the real VFX, directed by a deterministic timeline and rendered offline at integer scale. Same game, same pixels, framed like cinema.

Work in phases. Each phase ends at a **gate**: a rendered proof that WJ can look at.

---

## 0. The story (locked; this is the spine)

1. **Two worlds**, close enough to see each other's lights.
2. **The Witness** (the angry eye) looked at them, and where it looked they cracked.
3. **The Mouth** came along the edge of the dark and ate.
4. **The survivors** did not run. From the wreck and the two dead hearts they built **우리** ("we"): a body made of despair, built to give two murdered worlds one last fighting chance and someone to answer for it.
5. **우리 hunts** between the galaxies for a hungry mouth and an angry eye. Whatever stands in its way, it goes through.

### Recognisability rules
- **The eye IS the Witness.**
  - Use its real renderer: the round gold-rimmed lens, the celadon iris, the black pupil, the halo rings, the tentacle arms with gold studs, the dangling gold threads. Its gaze is its real beam attack.
  - Never show a generic eye.
- **The mouth IS the Mouth.**
  - Use the locked renderer and rig from the integration bundle: edge-anchored coils, crane neck, jaw-rings, teeth rows, slime threads.
  - Its strike in the story is its real crane strike.
- **The Planet renderer draws both doomed worlds** with two new palettes: world A temperate (blue and ochre), world B stranger (violet and teal). Add a city-lights layer on the night side. Same matter, so they crack exactly like the Planet does in a fight.
- **The two cores of 우리 are the two worlds' cores.** World A's core is hot orange; world B's is cold violet. Show this match visibly: the camera rhymes the shots.

## 2. Build: cinema mode in the engine

### 2.1 Architecture (new module `src/cinema/`)
- **`timeline.json`.** Shots with `[start, end]`, a camera track, actor tracks, VFX cues, cards, grade, audio cues and letterbox state. It is human-editable: WJ retimes the trailer by editing numbers.
- **`Director`.** Steps the simulation at a fixed 1/60 s with a seeded RNG (fully deterministic). It owns time remapping (slow-motion, freeze, speed ramps) per shot.
- **`CineCamera`.** Position, zoom, dutch angle, shake and parallax depth.
  - Zoom is continuous but **renders at integer scales whenever it is at rest**.
  - During a zoom, render at the next integer up and crop, so pixels never resample.
  - Parallax uses 3–4 depth layers (nebula far, dust mid, debris near, foreground particles).
- **Actors** are the **real titans**, driven by **scripted inputs** through the normal move system, so every attack is the real attack with real damage.
  - Pose overrides are allowed only for cinema-only beats (the genesis, the Witness close-up).
- **Cinema-only actors** use the same renderers and palettes:
  - `World` is the Planet renderer with palettes A and B plus a city-lights layer;
  - `Genesis` is 우리's rig parts with a per-chunk assembly track (port `story.js` → `drawUri` k-assembly);
  - `Ark` is an emissive point swarm.
- **MATTER CAM.** A render path that crops a native region (e.g. 40×17) and scales it ×16–×48. Each native pixel draws as a lit cell with its material, normal and damage state, so "every pixel is matter" is visibly true.
- **Grade.** Per-act palette LUTs in the master palette space (no free colour):
  - Before: cold blue;
  - the Witness: gold and celadon;
  - the Mouth: red, with blacks crushed;
  - Build: ember;
  - Hunt: neutral at full saturation;
  - Edge: desaturated, except the flesh.
- **Letterbox.** Pixel-exact bars, animatable (they shake with the camera and burst open at 0:46).
- **Cards.** Jersey 10, Silkscreen and DotGothic16, rasterised at native resolution with a 1-pixel dark outline, so they render with the same crispness as the sprites. No anti-aliasing at all.
- **Screen-space jaw wipe.** The Mouth's teeth rows are rendered as a full-frame iris that closes.

### 2.2 Offline render (`?cinema=trailer&render=1`)
- Step the sim per frame. Read back the native framebuffer and stream it to a local writer (a node script plus ffmpeg rawvideo, as in the v2 film kit).
- Output:
  - **3840×2160 (×6)** master;
  - **1920×1080 (×3)** delivery;
  - `-sws_flags neighbor`, x264 `-tune animation -crf 12`, plus ProRes 422 HQ for editing.
- **Motion blur** only on fast moves: accumulate 4–8 sub-frames **in native space before upscale**, gated per shot.
- **Determinism test.** The same frame rendered twice must give the same hash. CI renders 5 frames and compares them.

### 2.3 Audio: rendered by the game's own synth, in sync
- Render the game's WebAudio engine through an `OfflineAudioContext` driven by the same timeline, so every impact SFX lands sample-exact on its frame.
- **Score:** three leitmotifs.
  - **우리:** two notes, one per world, a fifth apart. It gains layers as the body is assembled.
  - **The Witness:** glassy and high, rings in close dissonance.
  - **The Mouth:** sub plus wet noise, no pitch.
- Stems: drone, motifs, percussion (the genesis drum hits are the weld cues), SFX and VO.
- Export stems as WAV so WJ can rescore or replace any of them.
- **VO:** one whispered Korean line: *우리는 아직 여기 있다* (we are still here). WJ's own voice, recorded dry. Fallback: no VO, subtitle only.
- **Silence #1** (after the bite) and **Silence #2** (before the punch) are hard silences: room tone only, no music tail.

## 3. Phases and gates

| Phase | Build | Gate (what WJ sees) |
|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Cinema mode skeleton: timeline, director, camera, letterbox, offline render, determinism test | A 5 s test render, deterministic (same hash twice) |
| **P1** | World A and B actors (Planet renderer, palettes, city lights) plus MATTER CAM | Stills: two worlds; a macro crust shot of crack, hold, shear |
| **P2** | The Witness close-up rig plus the gaze sweep using its real beam | **0:12–0:20 rendered**. Approval required: does it read as *the* Witness? |
| **P3** | The Mouth at the frame edge (locked renderer), crane strike on world B, bite, shake | **0:20–0:24 rendered** |
| **P4** | Genesis in-engine (port from `story.js`) with parallax, sparks VFX and drum cues | **0:24–0:46 rendered** |
| **P5** | Gameplay takes: scripted input tracks for 6 matchups; pick the best seeds | A contact sheet of 30 candidate shots |
| **P6** | Act III/IV assembly: bars bursting, name slams, scale pull-back, jaw wipe, title | **Full animatic at ×1** (fast render) |
| **P7** | Audio: offline synth render, score stems, VO slot, mix | Animatic with sound |
| **P8** | Final render: ×6 master, ×3 delivery, ProRes, the 30 s and 15 s cuts, hero loop | Deliverables |

Rules:
- Never upscale with smoothing.
- Never use colours outside the master palette (the grade LUTs map inside it).
- Never drop a frame (offline only).
- Keep `timeline.json` the single source of truth, and give every shot a comment saying what it is for.
