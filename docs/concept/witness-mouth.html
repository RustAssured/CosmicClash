The Witness, corrected

Left, a frame from your fight video. Right, the version the concept art asked for: the eye as the event, an irregular mosaic head with a burning gold socket, round cellular arms with one keen edge, a broken gold halo of nested rings, and an inverted city of spires hanging underneath.

The arms became legs

The concept’s arms are broad, curved blades with area. The rig was asked for jointed chains, so each arm became a line of segments, which the eye reads as legs.

The texture had nowhere to go

A painting carries meaning in its texture. At 640 by 360 that texture disappears, and only the silhouette is left. Nobody translated the silhouette, so the parts got their simplest shapes.

The proportions shrank

In the art the eye is a third of the body and the whole thing towers over the Planet. In the game it is the same size as the Planet, and the dark mass around the eye became a thin ring.

아귀, from your key art

The Witness is the eye of the universe. This one is its mouth. Its mouth is a tunnel of tooth-rings, each spinning on its own, receding into a body that passes through wounds in space. You never see where it begins or ends.

Earlier explorations

The three directions from before the key art, kept for comparison. The key art landed closest to C.

A. The wheel

Three rings of teeth, each spinning on its own axis, around a throat with stars of the wrong colours inside. Almost no body: the rest of it is pressure you can see bending the space around it.

Purest angel. Hardest to damage readably: there is very little body to carve.

B. The finger

A tear in space at the edge of the screen, and a segmented body coming through it, ending in a lamprey mouth with three rows of teeth. You never see where it ends.

Purest horror. Lots of body to cut, each severed segment keeps writhing.

C. The coil

The body of the finger with the wheel as its mouth. It comes through the rift, wraps behind the Planet and back in front: this is the grappler, and the reason the depth axis exists.

My pick. It keeps the angel, the horror and the off-screen scale, and its coils make the fight three-dimensional.
