The intact body 90–180 px across; map ≤ 224² (leave headroom where the titan grows).
3. **Secondary animation** (in `renderLayers`): every titan alive at rest — see per-titan notes.
4. **Behaviour**: unique mechanics through the `Behaviour` hook system; destruction signature expressed purely through `DamageEvent` type/shape/flags/params — **matchup physics must emerge from the matter simulation, not special cases** (do not special-case titan-vs-titan).
5. **Failure mode** implemented and visible (see below). **Resource** implemented with `parts` for the HUD.
6. **SimEvents & cues**: emit the standard events (move/charge/release/hit/shockwave/shake/…) sized by energy/mass, and `cue` events with these ids so audio can react: `tendril-sever` (Last One), `node-dark`, `harvest`, `chain-latch` (Nexus), `disk-shed`, `consume`, `hawking` (Black Hole), `fuel-burn`, `collapse`, `layer-blow` (Supernova), `moon-lost`, `crust-crack`, `atmo-strip`, `ocean-boil` (Planet).
7. **Tests**: JSON validation, generator determinism/invariants, feel measurements, hit resolution for the unique mechanics, failure-mode triggers, determinism of a scripted duel on the real world (state hash), plus a Node headless smoke duel vs an existing titan (`tools/ai/duel.ts`) that completes without exceptions or stalls.
8. **AI**: add the titan's personality (weights) and move metadata (`extra.recoil`, ranges, tags) so `createAI` plays it sensibly; run `tools/ai/duel.ts` against every existing titan at level 3 (n ≥ 20, sides alternated) — report results honestly. Do not tune for balance yet beyond making it non-degenerate (no stalls, no one-hit-KOs, fights end).
9. **Docs**: `docs/TITANS.md` regenerates from JSON (`tools/titans`), keep READMEs accurate.

## Pacing target (applies to everything you tune)

# Track A — THE NEXUS and BLACK HOLE

## The Nexus (De Nexus) 넥서스 — crimson graph of nodes and hardened chains around a crystalline hub; it grows
MASS 6 · COH 6 · HEAT 4 · GRAV 4 · REACH 8 · TEMPO 4. Resource: **nodes**. Destruction signature: **ASSIMILATION**.
- **Look**: a wireframe-crystal hub (faceted, translucent-feeling, hue-shifted magenta/ice with hard glints) at the core; radial and cross **chains** (hardened links: chunky iron-red segments with shading and rivets, visibly linked ovals — not lines) connecting **nodes** (bright crimson faceted beads with glowing cores) into a planar/3D-feeling graph ~130–170 px across; empty space between nodes is void (the body is a *lattice*, deliberately sparse). Silhouette must read at 64 px as "a crimson network around a crystal". Map larger than the intact body to leave room to grow.
- **Secondary animation**: graph pulses (light travelling along chains toward the hub), crystal refraction shimmer, nodes breathing, chains swaying by a few pixels via shear/overlay, loose link-ends whipping when a chain is cut.
- **Moves**: Strike **Latch** (a chain lashes out and hooks: ASSIMILATION `line`/point with `LATCH`, starts infection); Crush **Constrict** (chains wrap and pull: CRUSH+LATCH, drags the enemy in, "hardened chains root and slow" the target: apply a rooted/slowed status through the Fighter's standard status hook — a time-limited speed multiplier — while chains are attached); Surge **Slither** (a slow, wide, heavy glide — the Nexus's worst Surge by design, chains trail); Signature **Harvest** (hold to charge: tear out infected/assimilated lattice — ASSIMILATION with `harvest` param; matter flies to the Nexus hub and is **added to its own graph** via `world.grow(..., 'lattice'/'node')`; more nodes = more max resource); Ultimate **Ingesloten Oog** ("Enclosed Eye", 4 s): chains spiral out and **build a shell around the enemy** (a visible growing cage of chains and nodes, drawn as a fighter layer + collision `ring` shape); if completed (not broken by enough damage to the shell chains within the window) it **crushes inward** (heavy CRUSH ring + LATCH); Guard: a chain-mesh shell (great vs KINETIC, poor vs THERMAL as lattice burns).
- **Passive**: *Growth* — harvested mass permanently adds nodes/cells (visible growth, MASS attribute drifts up slowly).
- **Failure mode**: **edges snap, nodes go dark, disconnected islands of the graph die off.** Implement via matter connectivity: chain cells are the only bonds between nodes; a destroyed chain isolates a sub-graph → it detaches (matter world does this) and the Nexus must *also* mark its nodes dark (grey the emissive, lose their resource, drop `parts`) — islands die off (chunks that fade/crumble rather than tumble as healthy debris).
- **AI style**: builds, traps and encloses — sets up rooted targets, keeps chains attached, retreats to regrow, goes for Ingesloten Oog when the foe is slowed or cornered.

## Black Hole 블랙홀 — true-black horizon, photon ring, accretion disk (Doppler-brightened on one side), relativistic jets; lenses the background
MASS 8 · COH 9 · HEAT 10 · GRAV 10 · REACH 5 · TEMPO 2. Resource: **accreted mass** (bar). Destruction signature: **TIDAL CONSUMPTION**.
- **Look**: a perfectly black event-horizon disc (`horizon` archetype: immune to everything — *it cannot be hit*), a thin, brilliant **photon ring**, a tilted accretion disk of `diskGas` cells (hot orange-white on the approaching side, dim red on the receding side — Doppler beaming), thin relativistic jets along the axis (`jet` cells) with knotted structure. Disk cells are matter (they can be hit, torn, shed). Disk **rotation** is an overlay animation (streaks orbiting, brightness modulated per cell by angle) — matter maps don't rotate, so animate brightness/streak positions in `renderLayers` deterministically from tick. Set `FighterView.lensRadius/lensStrength` so the renderer lenses the scenery (strength grows with accreted mass). Big: ~150–190 px across including disk.
- **Secondary animation**: disk rotation + Doppler shimmer, photon-ring flicker, jet knots travelling outward, infalling particle motes, Hawking sparkle at low mass.
- **Moves**: Strike **Shear** (a short tidal pulse: TIDAL `field` small radius, pulls loose cells); Crush **Maw** (slow, huge: expanding tidal `field`/ring that peels the target's outer layers into streams that spiral into the disk); Surge **Slip** (a slow, heavy, gravity-bent drift, brief intangibility); Signature **Gravity Well** (hold to charge: `world.setGravitySource` with growing radius/strength pulling debris **and the opponent** (GRAV attribute) toward it; TIDAL continuous field on the target; mass consumed inside `consumeRadius` is credited to the Black Hole via `creditBodyId`) ; Ultimate **Spaghettification** (4 s: a colossal, screen-wide tidal field; the target is stretched and drawn into the disk); Guard: disk shield (a swirling gas shell that deflects KINETIC/FRACTURE, weak to THERMAL? — decide from materials).
- **Passive**: *Accretion* — as it consumes it grows heavier and stronger but **slower** (invert the usual stats-from-mass rule: `speedMul` down, `damageMul`/`mass` up as `massFrac` rises above 1; `world.grow(bodyId, {materialKey:'diskGas'})` converts pool→disk cells).
- **Failure mode**: **the horizon can't be hit — the disk can.** Damage aimed at the centre does nothing; a disrupted disk sheds mass (`world.shed`), the disk thins, the Black Hole shrinks and slows *less*; at low mass it **evaporates in Hawking sparkle** (final phase: horizon shrinks, sparkle particles, KO by evaporation).
- **Emergent matchups to verify by test (not special-case)**: ignited gas (Supernova/Planet atmosphere) pulled into the disk; burning debris streams; a Planet's atmosphere stripped.
- **AI style**: zones and pulls: keeps its distance, sets wells, lets the foe come, punishes with Maw when the foe is pinned.

# Track B — SUPERNOVA and PLANET

## Supernova 초신성 — a dying star in convulsions
MASS 7 · COH 3 · HEAT 10 · GRAV 6 · REACH 6 · TEMPO 5. Resource: **core fuel** (bar). Destruction signature: **THERMAL**.
- **Look**: a roiling star: **convection granulation** (cell-like bright/dark plasma grains, animated), sunspots, **prominences** (arcing loops of plasma), **corona filaments**, a pulsing white-hot core; bright, loud, hue-shifted (orange/red/white with violet shadow, a cool blue-white core flash at peak); ~130–160 px + corona/prominences. Layers: corona (`corona`) → outer plasma (`plasma`) → inner convective shell → `core` (`starCore`-like). Emissive: the brightest thing in the game — check bloom with B1's renderer (`tools/lead/shot.mjs` on the integrated app) that it doesn't wash out its own detail.
- **Secondary animation**: granulation churn, prominence arcs launching and re-falling, pulsing, flares; when fuel is low the star dims/reddens and shrinks.
- **Moves**: Strike **Flare** (a burst of heat: THERMAL cone; low fuel cost); Crush **Coronal Ejection** (a huge slow fireball / arc: THERMAL + `shock` param, blasts loosened material away, ignites); Surge **Solar Wind** (fast jet-propelled drift, brief intangibility, leaves a burning trail? — cosmetic + heat particles); Signature **Prominence** (hold-to-charge: an arcing loop of plasma that lands as a wall of fire — THERMAL `line`/ring, `CONTINUOUS`, burning fronts); Ultimate **Nova** (4 s: collapses inward then a full-body blast: a huge THERMAL ring + shock wave; costs meter *and* most fuel); Guard: plasma shell (great vs KINETIC/CRUSH? plasma is low-density; strong vs FRACTURE (nothing to shear), weak to TIDAL).
- **Fuel mechanic**: fuel powers moves **and shrinks the star** (`world.shed(bodyId, mass, 'burn')` proportional to fuel spent); at **zero fuel** it collapses into a **desperate final nova** (a scripted last Ultimate-like burst when fuel hits 0 and it has not yet been KO'd, with camera drama) and afterwards fights on as a dim, fragile remnant.
- **Passive**: *Radiance* — heat ambient: nearby enemy cells slowly heat (a small continuous THERMAL `field` around it, magnitude by fuel), and it recovers a little fuel when hit by KINETIC/THERMAL (absorbing energy) — decide and document.
- **Failure mode**: **outer layers blow off, exposing the core** — heavy damage to the star strips the outer plasma shell in big sheets (matter world handles removal; the Fighter marks `exposed core` and makes the core cells take more damage/vulnerable, visible bright core).
- **AI style**: bursts then retreats to cool (spend fuel in a flurry, back off to recover, never sit at zero fuel unnecessarily).

## Planet — a layered world with moons
MASS 8 · COH 7 · HEAT 6 · GRAV 7 · REACH 6 · TEMPO 3. Resource: **moons** (pips, 1–2). Destruction signature: **CRUSH & TECTONICS**.
- **Look**: a gorgeous layered world ~150–180 px: thin **atmosphere** halo (`gas`), swirling **clouds** (`cloud`), **oceans** (`ocean`), **continents** (rock/regolith/ice caps with coastlines and mountain ranges), **crust** → **mantle** → glowing **magma core** (`magma`/`core`) — damage must *reveal* these strata (craters expose mantle, deep craters reveal glowing magma). Terminator lighting from the stage key light, city-less but wild: storms, ice caps. **1–2 moons** are separate small bodies (`world.createBody({kind:'moon'})`, ~20–34 px, cratered) **orbiting** on visible elliptical orbits (parallax: pass in front of/behind the planet); they are its weapons and shields.
- **Secondary animation**: cloud drift (animated overlay), moon orbits, aurora/atmospheric glow, storm swirls, tectonic glow in cracks.
- **Moves**: Strike **Tidal Nudge** (a gravitational shove: CRUSH point/field, knockback); Crush **Impact** (the planet's mass slammed: CRUSH with `compress`/`crater` params and a shock ring — craters with shock rings); Surge **Orbital Shift** (slow, ponderous but with intangibility; a moon whips around); Signature **Moon Slam** (hold to aim: a moon is flung at the target: KINETIC/CRUSH projectile with the moon's mass; the moon returns or, if it hits hard, is spent; spends the *moons* resource); Ultimate **Cataclysm** (4 s: gravity compression + tectonic quake: CRUSH field on the target, tectonic crack lines, then a moon barrage); Guard: **atmosphere shield** (absorbs THERMAL strongly via `heatAbsorb`, moons swing in front as physical blockers).
- **Passive**: *Gravity Well* — pulls nearby debris into slow orbit around it (matter gravity source, small): debris orbits as a ring of remains (cosmetic + occasional KINETIC on the enemy).
- **Failure mode**: **crust cracks reveal glowing magma; atmosphere is stripped; moons can be knocked out of orbit and lost.** Moons have integrity and can be hit/knocked (they intercept blows); a lost moon drifts away as a debris chunk (`cue moon-lost`, resource −1); atmosphere stripping removes the `gas` shell (reduces heat absorption); the magma glow spreads through crack lines as the crust breaks.
- **Emergent matchup facts to verify by test**: atmosphere absorbs heat (a Supernova's THERMAL does far less to an intact Planet than to a stripped one); oceans boil away when burned; a Black Hole strips atmosphere first.
- **AI style**: fortress — keeps moons as shields, sits back, punishes with Impact and Moon Slam, uses gravity to control space.
