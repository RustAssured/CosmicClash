## Mission
Give the roster a body, a will and a fighting style. (1) **Procedurally sculpt** each titan at load into a detailed pixel matter map that *reads at 64 px* and whose interior layers **reveal** as damage carves it;
(2) build the **Fighter**: a responsive-yet-weighty state machine with input buffering, aim, charge, feint-cancel, Guard, knockback, meter, resource, live stats from mass, and pixel-accurate hit resolution against
the matter world; (3) give every titan its **moveset in data files** with frame data; (4) build the **AI** that reads space, exploits damage and punishes.
This phase is the **vertical slice: THE LAST ONE vs THE ASTEROID** — but the Fighter/Art/AI framework must be built so the other four titans are *content* (Phase 2), not new engineering.

## 2. The Fighter (`src/combat`)
`createFighter(opts: FighterOptions): Fighter` (contract in `sim.ts`). Everything the Match needs and nothing more; Fighter owns: body creation (`world.createBody`), per-tick transform, movement, moves, hit resolution, resource/meter, Guard, KO, `FighterView` (mutated in place; **no per-tick allocation**), `renderLayers`, `debugShapes`, `nextRound`.
- **Input model** (`InputFrame`): stick aims (up / forward / down by stick angle at press time; forward = neutral or toward foe). Buttons: STRIKE, CRUSH, SURGE, SIGNATURE (hold to charge where the move allows), ULTIMATE (meter ≥ 1), GUARD (hold), FEINT (cancel windup).
  **Input buffer 9 ticks** (`INPUT_BUFFER_TICKS`); windups can be **cancelled into Guard or Surge during the first `cancelWindow` (≤40%) of startup**; FEINT cancels windup with a recoverable stumble (short). The **first visible response is on the same tick** as the press (anticipation pose / charge light / lean) and `SimEvent {t:'move'}` fires that tick.
- **Feel (measurable — write tests that measure them)**: reach ~70% of top speed within 0.2 s of stick input (first-order response τ≈0.166 s), then a long glide (half-life ≈ 0.35 s) on release; direction changes show **inertia** (`transform.lean`, tendrils/debris trail) but stay controllable; top speed scales with TEMPO/mass (start around 150–260 px/s and tune by feel).
  Soft arena walls, tether (`MAX_FIGHTER_DX/DY` — the app relies on it; never let the fighters exceed it), an altitude spring toward `arena.restY`, pixel-mask body separation (fighters may slightly overlap edges but never pass through each other's cores).
  Hit-stop 60–220 ms scaled by energy and both masses (emit `hitstop` SimEvent + `freeze()` visuals); knockback = impulse / effective mass, with hit-stun that is **short for heavy titans** (never a stun-lock; guarantee an escape route: Surge is always available out of stun after a brief window).
  Emit `shake`, `zoom`, `roll`, `shockwave`, `flash`, `rumble`, `timescale` (KO blow 0.3×) `SimEvent`s sized by energy/mass — the camera/renderer/audio/rumble are driven from these. Ultimates emit `ultimate start/end` and hold camera-friendly poses for 3–5 s.
- **Hit resolution** (pixel-accurate): each active hitbox becomes a world-space `DamageShape`; attacker calls `foe.probe(shape)`; if it overlaps, build a `DamageEvent` (energy from the move × `stats.damageMul` × charge, `sourceMass`, `originX/Y`, `sourceBodyId`, flags/params) and call `foe.receive(ev, attacker, events)`.
  `receive`: Guard shell absorbs a fraction depending on **damage type vs the shell's material** (each titan's guard has a material profile; sustained CRUSH drains `guardHealth` and breaks it → `guardbreak` stun), parts intercept (tendrils, moons, orbiting fragments — they have integrity and can be severed/knocked off), then `world.applyDamage(bodyId, ev)`, then knockback/hit-stun/meter/events.
  **Regional targeting**: the AI/human aims the blow (up/forward/down); already-damaged regions are weaker (the matter world reports `onDamagedFraction`) — surface that in `SimEvent hit.onDamaged` for feedback.
- **Live stats from remaining mass** (`EffectiveStats` from `bodyStats.massFrac`): losing matter ⇒ lighter/faster (`speedMul`, `tempoMul` up) but weaker (`damageMul`, `mass` down); the Black Hole (Phase 2) inverts. `integrityPct` per `FighterView` docs, KO at `KO_CORE_INTEGRITY` / `KO_MASS_FRAC`.
- **Meter** fills from dealing **and receiving** damage (receiving fills less); **Resource** per titan (Last One: tendrils = also the shield of the eye; Asteroid: fragments).
- **Failure modes** (Phase 1 needs the two): **Last One** — tendrils sever one by one when their segments are hit (integrity per tendril; severed ones fall away as debris via `world.spawnChunk`/particles; `parts` = tendrils left; **the eye's incoming damage multiplier rises as tendrils are lost — the eye is exposed and takes heavy damage**; Guard = tendrils curl around the eye and thin out as they die).
  **Asteroid** — fragments shed with mass; below ~35% mass it becomes a **rubble pile**: lower `body.cohesionScale`, looser look, weaker hits, still fast.
- **Secondary animation** in `renderLayers`: verlet tendrils (pooled arrays, fixed iterations, stable, they react to the body's acceleration and to blows), halo shimmer, eye tracking the foe, asteroid pebbles/dust; anticipation/recovery poses via lean, squash-free integer shear, tendril posture, emissive pulses.
- `RenderLayer`s: body layer wraps `body.map.pixels/emissive/version/dirty` (no copy), overlay layers rasterised by you into preallocated buffers. Debug: `debugShapes` for Training.

### Movesets (design intent — tune frame data by feel; all in JSON)
**The Last One** — *"charge, and sidestep."* Strike **Tendril Lash** (arc of 3–5 tendril whips, FRACTURE `line` sweeps, carves a groove; total ≈ 38 ticks) · Crush **Shatter Blow** (eye flares, body lunges, FRACTURE point/cone with `SEED_CRACK` — a crack network spreads over the next seconds; startup 30, total ≈ 84) ·
Surge **Sidestep** — *the best Surge in the game*: fast 8-way glide with a long intangible window, afterimage of tendrils, very short recovery, can be entered from the first 40% of any windup, and a Strike input at its tail becomes a lunging strike (the "charge" half of the identity) · Signature **Gaze**: hold to charge (≤ 60–70 ticks), release a piercing beam
(FRACTURE `line`, `PIERCE|CONTINUOUS`, ~20 ticks) that **cuts a channel** through the target · Ultimate **The Last Light** (3.5–4.5 s: halo ignites, tendrils rise, the world dims and slows, a sweeping piercing beam + shear along weakest bonds) · Guard: tendril shell · Passive: patient regrowth (a tendril regrows when un-hit for a while) — name it in JSON.
**The Asteroid** — *hit-and-run.* Strike **Glancing Blow** (quick shoulder check, KINETIC point; total ≈ 32) · Crush **Meteor Strike** (pull back, full-mass ram, deep crater, `EMBED` shrapnel that fractures outward `embedDelay` ticks later; startup 26, total ≈ 80) ·
Surge **Tumble** (fast roll, short intangible, best for kiting) · Signature **Swarm**: split into a controlled swarm of fragments you steer with the stick for ~2 s (each a small KINETIC projectile; spends the *fragments* resource, survivors re-merge) · Ultimate **Kessler Cascade** (the body bursts into a storm that sweeps the arena, then reforms and slams) · Guard: regolith shell (great vs KINETIC, poor vs THERMAL) · Passive: **Momentum** (consecutive hits speed it up).

## 3. AI (`src/ai`) — *after the Fighter works end to end*
`createAI(level: Difficulty, def: TitanDef, seed: number): AIController` (contract in `ai.ts`). Utility-based decisions + a **short-horizon lookahead**: evaluate candidate actions by forward-simulating a few ticks with a lightweight kinematic + frame-data model (positions, move phases, hit windows, expected damage weighted by the foe's
`regionGrid`, exposure of your own damaged regions) — do **not** clone the full sim. Manages spacing, respects enemy windups (`foe.threats`, `foe.phase`), feints, punishes recovery, targets weakened regions, retreats when low. **Human-like:** observation delayed by `REACTION_MS[level]` (ring buffer of `FighterView` snapshots), imperfect execution (timing jitter, occasional misses, level-scaled blunder rate),
adaptation to the player's habits via n-gram prediction of their action sequence. **It must never read inputs before they happen** (it only sees public views; the observation is delayed). Personalities from `def.ai.weights`: Last One patient, sidesteps before punishing · Asteroid hit-and-run (dash in, strike, tumble out).
Levels 1–5 + 6 "Titan". `AIController.log` keeps a ring buffer of human-readable decision notes ("punish: recovery frame 14, foe crush whiff") for the review loop. `tools/ai/duel.ts` (run with `npx tsx`): headless duel runner that plays N fights between two titans at given levels and prints results (needs the Lead's `src/sim/match.ts` — when it lands, use it; before that drive fighters directly).
Balance tournament (`tools/ai/balance.ts`, `worker_threads`, ≥100 fights/matchup, win-rate matrix → `docs/BALANCE.md`) is Phase 3; scaffold `duel.ts` so it is a small step later.

Feel measurements (input→first-visible-response ≤ 1 tick; 70%-speed time; buffer window; cancel window; hit-stop clamp). Generator determinism + invariants (core inside body, all materials referenced exist, connected body, size within 90–180 px, ids valid, bonds not required). AI never uses the foe's pending input (static test: `decide` only takes `AIContext`), respects the reaction delay (measure), and beats a do-nothing dummy at level ≥ 2.
