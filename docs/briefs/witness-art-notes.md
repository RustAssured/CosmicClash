## Change in Phase 2 (priority order)

1. **Make the silhouette vertical, not a wide symmetric ∞.**
   - v0 is 280×175: the four arms spread horizontally and read as a crab or an ornamental clock.
   - The concept is a hanging throne: sigil and halo above, the eye cradle in the middle, the chandelier below.
   - Arms are long scythe architectures that sweep down and around (and asymmetrically up), like the concept's tendril-architecture curling below the body.
   - Target a bounding box roughly as tall as it is wide or taller; width ≤ ~200 px at rest. The 640-px view must leave room for orbital footwork.
2. **The shrine mass is silhouette element #4.**
   - The v0 gold collar with short spikes is too small.
   - Build a downward chandelier of 5–9 MAJOR bone/gold spires, the longest hanging well below the carapace (≈ 30–40 % of total height).
   - Make it asymmetric in length, with one or two snapped short. Spires snap off as damage.
3. **The halo must be a large BROKEN ring around the body**, clearly bigger than the carapace (radius ≈ 1.3–1.5× the carapace).
   - 3–5 arc segments with gaps, orbiting independently, plus the sigil above.
   - In v0 the arcs sit almost on the carapace and vanish at 64 px.
4. **"Wrong at the second glance."**
   - v0 is too tidy and symmetric, which makes it a little cute.
   - Add subtle wrongness:
     - asymmetric arm lengths and joint counts;
     - a halo segment slightly out of plane or out of phase;
     - the pupil not quite centred (it tracks);
     - a few hairline cyan fractures that glow as if something inside is looking out;
     - orb-eyes that are dormant but not quite closed.
   - Never goofy or demonic.
5. **The 64 px thumbnail must read** as eye + broken halo + hanging spires. Check this every iteration.
6. **Cell budget:** v0 has 25.5k cells. With one body per region that is acceptable, but keep the total ≤ ~26k.

## Microdetail: what the final version would need (S2 note, proposal only)

**Where it stands.** The eye now carries in-pixel microdetail (`paintWitnessEye`, `micro` on by default):
- sclera: ice-plate boundaries, latitude seams, five fissures, a crenellated ring wall at the limbus;
- iris: 14 canyons, three terrace lips, three warm settlement lights, a collarette with 16 built slots;
- pupil: a shaft with stepped rims and a deep glow at the bottom;
- highlight: a lens facet with a seam.

Every mark is a whole ramp step on the dithered level. The material map is identical, so silhouette, matter and hit masks don't change. Measured with `tools/titans/witness-eye.ts`, the 1× read (3×3 blur) moves 2.8 luma on average. Sheets: `docs/gallery/wip/witness-eye-micro-{1x,3x,4x-crop}.png`.

**Is in-pixel detail at 480×270 enough?**
- For play distance and for the ×2–×4 aftermath zoom of the same pixels, yes. The eye is 56 px across, so a 1-px canyon or wall reads as structure at ×4.
- It does not scale beyond that. At ×4 a "settlement" is still one 4×4 square, and nothing finer than a pixel exists to zoom into.
- The illusion WJ describes ("only from a great distance does it look like an eye") ultimately wants detail BELOW the play pixel.

**What the final version would need: a detail map for close-ups.**
1. **Art (S2):**
   - Paint the eye (and later the carapace, halo and spires) a second time at 4× resolution: a 224×224 eye detail map.
   - Same palettes and the same value structure, generated by the same painter at 4× sampling, so it stays consistent with the 1× map by construction.
   - About 1 day.
   - Cost: eye generation time scales with pixel count, so about 16× the 1× eye, once per match (not measured yet). Memory is about 200 KB RGBA per Witness (224×224×4). Generated, not shipped, so asset size stays 0.
2. **Render (S5):**
   - The aftermath and export paths draw the detail map in place of the 1× pixels inside the zoom window.
   - Only when the zoom is at least ×3. The rig part's transform (`rot` step, facing) is applied to the detail map, using the existing rotation sampler at 4× density.
   - Damage must still show, so mask the detail map with the live matter map: a destroyed cell shows the 1× wound/debris colour, not intact detail.
   - About 1–2 days.
3. **Contracts (Lead):**
   - An optional `detail?: { scale: 4; pixels: Uint32Array; w: number; h: number }` on `RenderPart` or `RenderLayer`.
   - Renderers that ignore it keep working.
   - One field, no simulation impact (render only, not hashed).
4. **Not touched:** matter, combat, simulation, determinism. Detail is purely presentational.

**Recommendation.** Keep in-pixel microdetail as the baseline; it already pays off at the ×2–×4 aftermath zoom. Build the detail map only if the aftermath gets a deeper zoom (×6+) or a photo/export mode.
