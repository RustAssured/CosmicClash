# CREATURE BRIEF: THE CRADLE / 태반체

From WJ, 2026-10-13. The key art is [keyart.webp](keyart.webp): a mood and silhouette reference, not a sprite to trace.

**Name:** THE CRADLE / 태반체 (*Taebanche*, roughly "placental body / entity")

**Essence:** gestation

**Design compass:** The Cradle is not a giant organism. It is a giant developmental process that happens to be fighting you.

## Silhouette
- A gigantic tilted fleshy disc, like a torn umbrella or an eclipsing placenta.
- Thick vascular spokes converge on a hanging central body-knot.
- Underneath trails one enormous segmented umbilical tail, ending in a blunt, jaw-like organ.
- At 64 px it must read immediately: **wide disc + hanging central mass + one long trailing cord.**

## Size class
**Large**, about 230–250 px. The membrane can temporarily take up far more screen space than the core body, so it feels larger than its actual collision mass.

## Materials (outside to inside)
- dark translucent membrane stretched between calcified rib-spokes;
- wet vascular tissue and branching capillaries;
- pale collagen cords;
- bruise-purple muscle sheets;
- mineralised bone nodules embedded like islands;
- internal sacs of dimly glowing amniotic fluid;
- deep inside, a slowly contracting black-red embryonic core. It is not an eye, a heart or any conventional organ.

Anything it consumes starts developing recognisable but incorrect anatomy inside the membrane.

It must look both biological and astronomically old. Not "big flesh monster", but an organism whose tissues evolved at planetary scale.

## Palette mood: placental dusk
**Colours:**
- dried burgundy;
- almost-black violet;
- sickly pearl;
- translucent amber;
- desaturated mauve;
- very sparse cold turquoise bioluminescence, only inside the fluid sacs.

**Avoid:** 우리's cyan machinery, the Last One's celadon/gold, the Planet's blue/green, and the Mouth's dominant wet-black/red tooth palette. The Witness is amethyst on violet, so keep the Cradle reading warm burgundy, not purple.

The overall impression is warm, suffocating, internal, womb-like darkness. Cold light appears only where the anatomy is failing.

## How it destroys matter: it GESTATES matter
It doesn't simply eat or crush. Contact tissue sends branching vascular tendrils into whatever it touches: rock, armour, atmosphere, energy structures. All of it becomes nutrient and developmental material.

**What happens to matter:** it is vascularised, softened, folded inward, and reorganised into useless biological structures. For example:
- a damaged asteroid sprouts cartilage-like ridges before it collapses;
- a section of 우리 grows temporary capillary webs over exposed structure;
- planetary crust bulges into malformed, organ-like masses.

**Its own wounds:** damage ruptures membrane layers and exposes nested developmental chambers. Some hold half-formed organs that clearly belong to no known species.

## Signature move: FALSE BIRTH
1. The Cradle spreads its disc until it dominates the arena. Vascular filaments lock onto the opponent from several directions.
2. The membrane contracts. For a moment the opponent disappears behind it.
3. The membrane violently opens again and expels a huge, malformed pseudo-organ grown from stolen matter. It is shaped partly by the victim it touched, and it becomes both a projectile and a temporary obstacle on the battlefield.

The frightening part is not that it made something. It is that for several seconds the game suggests the opponent was being used as developmental instructions.

## Weakness: differentiation failure
The Cradle survives because every part of its anatomy keeps telling neighbouring tissue what it is supposed to become. When it is heavily damaged, those instructions fail. Instead of simply bleeding, it develops incorrectly:
- membrane becomes bone;
- bone becomes vascular tissue;
- muscle calcifies;
- sacs grow where the structural supports should be.

Its silhouette progressively loses its elegant radial order and becomes a catastrophic biological knot. At low health it is in runaway self-gestation.

| State | Look |
|---|---|
| Healthy | horrible but organised |
| Damaged | proliferating |
| Near death | its own biology no longer knows what creature it is |

**Example:** knock out one radial spoke. Rather than leaving a plain hole, the neighbouring membrane spends the next ten seconds trying to repair it and accidentally grows a huge calcified organ there instead. You haven't just damaged the sprite; you've changed what its body thinks it is.

## Sound
- enormous wet sail tension;
- a subsonic whale pulse;
- distant, ultrasound-like rhythmic clicking;
- cartilage cracking under pressure;
- fluid shifting through pipes hundreds of kilometres wide;
- when it attacks: a deep contraction sound, not a roar.

Its voice should sound like listening to something alive through the wall of an impossible body.

## Lore
- **Main line:** "Nothing has ever seen The Cradle born. Every civilization that studied it eventually reached the same conclusion: it is not the child. It is the organ preparing the universe for one."
- **Short card line:** "It does not consume worlds. It prepares them."

## Homage notes
**Conceptual echoes:**
- Akira: uncontrolled bodily proliferation;
- Cronenberg: anatomy losing its categories;
- The Thing: biological identity becoming unreliable;
- cosmic embryology and teratoma imagery;
- deep-sea filter organisms and placental vasculature.

**It must stay its own creature, so avoid:**
- humanoid mutation;
- recognisable human fetus imagery;
- tentacle-monster shorthand;
- a giant mouth;
- eyeballs;
- a "flesh blob" silhouette.

**Its visual language:** radial membrane + vascular architecture + developmental chambers + one immense umbilical structure.

## Template
**blob.** Do not derive it from the Planet, Nexus, Last One, Mouth or 우리: it needs a completely fresh skeleton and motion model.

## Why it is strong (WJ)
- **Its gameplay verb, anatomy and horror are the same idea.** The Planet fights in gravity, 우리 in structural coherence, the Mouth in predatory reach. The Cradle fights in development itself.
- **Its silhouette is unlike anything else on the roster.**
- **Its failure mode is unique.** It doesn't get less capable as it is damaged; it gets less biologically certain. This is a chance to show off the persistent-damage system.
