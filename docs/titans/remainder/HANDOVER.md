# The Remainder: handover (for a voxel proof of concept)

Written 2026-10-25 for a separate thread that wants to try the Remainder in **voxels**. Everything here is in this repo
(`RustAssured/CosmicClash`) on the branch **`ccr-973eebc0-0j5u8j`** (not merged to `main` yet; the latest commit when this
was written is `5d7c2de`). Check that branch out: on `main` the Remainder does not exist yet.

Read first: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) (the design, every move, balance), [keyart.webp](keyart.webp) (the source art),
[clips/](clips/) (how it looks and moves now), [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md) (what is still unverified).

## 1. What it is

**THE REMAINDER / 잔여체** (`remainder`, ADK-010, shipped 2026-10-25). Kind: celestial. Size class: large (163×230 px at game
size). Home stage: `tussenruimte`.

> A wound in reality that learned to fear closing.

Born when spacetime tore open, it became self-aware and keeps itself open by force. It wants to close; it is afraid to.

**The design, in three rules (keep these in any version):**

1. **A rigid shell with violence inside.** The outer body (bone and crust) never swings, leans or lunges. Every attack
   erupts out of the vortex in the middle. The contrast is the character.
2. **Under constant strain.** The vortex swirls violently and drains inward, spasms shut and tears itself open again,
   sucks in the shell around it and the debris near it. The body twitches; it shakes when hit (the fear).
3. **Every button reads as a different action.** Snap (a rubber pull of the whole vortex), Inhale (a yank and a bite),
   Fold (it drains into its own eye and unfolds elsewhere), Compaction (crushes matter into a slug and fires it), Loosening
   (lets go: the wound tears open and slams shut), Hold Shut (an iris of bone closes over the wound).

The user's own feedback that shaped it: the vortex must "really swirl violently", "feel under stress, and very violent";
the moves should "contrast its look"; Snap must read as "pulling on a piece of rubber that's tightened around a circle",
and the stretch must be "the actual vortex pixels extending", not a graphic over it. The user loves Fold ("the
telepottation").

## 2. How the game is built (what a voxel POC has to fit into, or replace)

- **TypeScript + Vite**, rendering through **Three.js (WebGL2)** used as a thin wrapper (`src/render/`, see its README).
  The game draws a **640×360 logical frame**, upscaled by whole pixels. Everything on screen is a 2D `RenderLayer`
  (`src/contracts/render.ts`): a w×h RGBA sprite (+ optional per-pixel emissive for bloom) placed in world space with a
  facing (mirror) and a z order.
- **The sim is 2D cells.** Each titan's body is a `MatterMap` (`src/contracts/matter.ts`): a grid of cells, each with a
  material id, density, integrity, temperature, bonds to the right and down neighbours, infection, flags, and a pristine
  colour. Damage (KINETIC, CRUSH, TIDAL, THERMAL, FRACTURE, ASSIMILATION) chips, cracks, burns and detaches cells; the
  KO is mass below 22 % or the core below 25 %. Bonds between **different** materials are ×0.62 weaker, so bodies crack
  along material seams.
- **The sim is deterministic and netplayed.** 60 fixed ticks a second, lockstep. In `src/combat`, `src/titans`, `src/matter`
  and `src/sim` a lint rule forbids `Math.sin/cos/atan2/hypot/pow/exp` (use `DMath.*` from `src/contracts/dmath.ts`, or
  `fsin`/`fcos` from `src/combat/fx/idle.ts`). **Presentation is separate (S19):** the idle animation and move visuals only
  read the sim and never feed back, so they are free to be anything, and they are where a voxel renderer would plug in.

## 4. The body data (the input to voxelise)

`body.json` is an `ImageBody` (`src/titans/kit/image.ts`):

```json
{ "version": 1, "w": 171, "h": 238, "cells": "<base64, w×h bytes, row-major>", "core": { "x": 105.2, "y": 111.6, "r": 10 } }
```

Each byte: **material id in the high nibble** (1–15, 0 = empty), **light level 0–15 in the low nibble** (the step within
that material's colour ramp, 0 darkest). The material id is the index into `materials` in remainder.json, plus one.
`decodeCells(body)` returns `{ mat, level }` (Uint8Arrays). About 21,900 solid cells. Facing right; the core (the KO
anchor, the eye) is at (105, 112), radius 10.

| id | key | role | ramp (dark → light) | emissive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | crust | the dark outer shell | `#130e0a` … `#82707a` (7) | 0 |
| 2 | rubble | broken shell, deepest shadows | `#060302` … `#563e4d` (7) | 0 |
| 3 | membrane | the skin between shell and wound | `#29251c` … `#c4a9b5` (7) | 0 |
| 4 | bone | the pale plates (Hold Shut's iris is made of these) | `#40462c` … `#efe2d7` (7) | 0 |
| 5 | vortex | the wound: the swirl | `#211127` … `#ba64c2` (7) | 0 |
| 6 | ichor | green veins racing into the eye | `#31581d` … `#b4de77` (6) | 17 |
| 7 | spark | violet light threads in the vortex | `#421f4d` … `#e598e2` (6) | 15 |
| 8 | core | the eye (KO anchor), dense and tough | `#02030e` … `#9a42ad` (6) | 60 |

The level nibble is the cell's position on its material's ramp (`level / 15 × (ramp.length − 1)`), which the painter
(`paintImage`) relights with the stage light: `art.params.form` (0.25) adds a gentle dome and `dither` (0.35) blends ramp
steps. The only depth in the data is that dome: `map.height` rises with distance from the body's edge
(`1 + form · 16 · √(edgeDistance / 22)`), the same for every material. It says nothing about the anatomy (the vortex is
not recessed, the bone plates are not raised), so a voxel version must author its depth (§7).

The vortex's working radius in the code is the 85th percentile of vortex-cell distances from the core (`rv`, about 55 px).
The vortex, ichor, spark and core cells inside it are "the turning set" that swirls.

## 5. How it moves now (the reference to match)

All of it is presentation, driven by the sim tick. Watch [clips/remainder-idle.mp4](clips/remainder-idle.mp4) and
[clips/remainder-moves-reel.mp4](clips/remainder-moves-reel.mp4) first.

**Idle** (`remainderIdle.ts`):
- **Swirl as a flow map:** each turning cell shows the painted colour from where the spiral was a moment ago, further round
  (rim 2.4 rad/s, up to 5× faster near the eye) and further out (inflow 0.35/s, logarithmic). Two copies of the motion half
  a cycle (0.6 s) apart, cross-faded per cell by a hash threshold, so it turns forever without winding the arms into
  rings. Turbulence: one noise value per whole radius per frame.
- **Spasms:** every few seconds the eye squeezes shut and the swirl chokes (`close`), then it forces itself open
  (`open`: eye blown wide, a flash through the veins, a burst of speed, the body shudders).
- **Feeding:** the shell ring (rv … 1.6 rv) creeps inward, stress arcs crackle on the rim, wreckage chunks orbit in and are
  consumed, dust motes stream over the shell into the eye; ichor races down the veins.
- **Strain:** random twitches; shakes on hits and spasms (whole-pixel body jitter).
- The body is redrawn every second tick.

**Moves** (frame data: startup / active / recovery, in ticks at 60/s):

| Slot | Move | Ticks | The look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strike | Snap | 9 / 4 / 26 | the front of the vortex disc warped toward the aim like a rubber sheet: its rim leaves the circle on the two tangent lines to the tip, the arms and the eye drag into streaks, ripples run along it; it cracks at the tip, snaps back, the vortex dents and wobbles on a damped spring. Reach 166 px. |
| Crush | Inhale | 28 / 12 / 50 | the tell (spins up, darkens, the eye widens, a cone of dust rushes in), the yank, two toothed bone mandibles bite at the rim |
| Surge | Fold | 4 / 14 / 16 | the body twists down into its eye, a tear closes where it was, it unfolds from a second tear (intangible 2–16) |
| Signature | Compaction | 14 / 6 / 26, hold ≤ 60 | compression rings close on the eye, a slug grows; fired forward/up (muzzle ring, comet trail) or ground into bone plates when aimed down |
| Ultimate | Loosening | 40 / 150 / 50 | the wound tears open across the shell, light cracks run out, everything is dragged in; slams shut (bone iris, ring, shockwave) |
| Guard | Hold Shut | 3 / 0 / 5 | seven bone petals close over the vortex from the rim inward, seams of light, a slit at the centre |

**Snap's warp (the piece the user cared most about)** is `drawSnap` in `remainderFx.ts`: for each pixel in the tongue's
bounding box, find its coordinate along the aim (`along`) and across it (`lat`); the outline half-width is the circle's
(`√(R² − along²)`) up to the tangent point `bx = R²/d`, then a concave band `hw0 · (1 − s)^1.35` to the tip; the source
pixel is found by compressing `along` from [0.3R, d] back into [0.3R, R] (`k = 1 − (1 − g)^1.6`, so the tip stretches most)
and scaling `lat` to the disc's width there. It samples the idle's **own live pixels** (`RemainderIdle.skin`). In 3D this
is a lattice/free-form deformation of the vortex toward a moving tip, which is a natural fit for voxels or a shader.

**Gameplay** (unchanged by any rendering): Snap tears pixels off the foe and drags them in; Inhale pulls the foe for 8
ticks; Mass (0–5 pips) is earned from what it tears off and from blows swallowed by Hold Shut, and spent by Compaction;
Loosening exposes the core (a blow reaching the eye lands twice); below a third of its mass it turns **Cannibal** (eats its
own shell inward, faster, weaker guard). Balance: 65 % against the roster at AI level 4.

## 6. Running and checking it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev                         # then http://localhost:5173/
#   fight it:   /?mode=aivai&a=remainder&b=lastone&stage=tussenruimte&seed=21&ai=4
#   play it:    /?mode=training&a=remainder&b=planet
#   builder:    /dev/builder/  (pick The Remainder; ▶ Studio plays any move frame by frame with hitboxes)
npx vitest run src/combat/remainder.test.ts src/combat/behaviours/kit   # its tests
npm run titan:check -- remainder --quick                               # size, palette, AI, replay
npx tsx tools/titans/clip.ts out.mp4 --a=remainder --b=lastone --cam=0 --seconds=4 --script0="20:strike"   # a clip, no GPU
```

The software compositor (`dev/titans/blit.ts`, used by `tools/titans/clip.ts` and the builder's studio) draws the same
layers as the game without a GPU: useful in a cloud sandbox, where the WebGL renderer does not start.

## 7. Notes for the voxel POC

**Two ways in, cheapest first:**

1. **Presentation only (recommended first).** Keep the 2D sim and its determinism; build a voxel *view* of the body and
   draw it instead of (or beside) the body layer. Each solid cell becomes a column of voxels; the material and the cell's
   live state (`map.material`, `map.integrity`, `map.flags`, `map.pixels`) say what is there each frame, so damage, chips
   and detachment show for free. The idle and moves keep working as data: the idle already computes, per cell, where its
   colour comes from (the flow map), which can drive the voxel colours or a shader. Plug-in point: the behaviour's
   `renderLayers()` (`RemainderBehaviour` in `remainder.ts`), or a separate Three.js scene for the POC.
2. **A 3D body in the sim.** Much bigger: the matter world, bonds, damage shapes, hit tests and netplay hashing are all 2D.
   Only worth it once (1) shows the look is right.

**Depth has to be authored** (the data only has the edge dome). A starting heightmap by material, matching the key art: the shell (crust,
rubble, bone, membrane) thick and raised, highest on the bone plates; the vortex **recessed** as a funnel that deepens
toward the eye (depth ∝ 1 − r/rv, the core the deepest point); ichor and spark as thin inlays riding the funnel. The
funnel is what makes the swirl read as a hole in reality rather than a painted disc. The key art (keyart.webp) is the
reference for which parts stand out.

**Keep:** the rigid-shell-violent-inside contrast; the palette and ramps (they are tuned against every other titan's
signature colours: accent `#8dc155`, accent2 `#b35fb8`); the pixel-art read at 640×360 (voxels around one game pixel in
size, nearest filtering, no smooth shading that blurs the ramps); the timings in §5 (they are balanced). **Free to
change:** everything in remainderIdle.ts and remainderFx.ts is presentation and can be rebuilt for voxels.

**Known open items** (not voxel-related): the real-game pass in [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md) has not been done on a GPU;
its fights measure about −17.5 LUFS against the −20 target (from the matter they tear loose); the site hover clip, key-art
lineup and a CLASSIC ladder slot are left by hand.
